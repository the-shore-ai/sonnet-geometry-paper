## Verification scope and residual limits

The public compact verifier must recalculate the identity/geometry table from lossless pooled vectors, all frozen criteria from retained RDMs, line/token contrasts including omissions, and the selected shared-response decomposition. Its receipt must identify checked quantities and tolerate only stated numerical differences. Historical source assertions remain backed by retained primary records and independent numerical audits; this release does not create missing historical diagnostics or supply an external reproduction of the entire private archive.

Full token-level tensors and complete modern editorial/HTML snapshots are excluded. Source-retrieval recipes bind exact raw hashes and generated normalized texts. A source change fails closed. The Spenser Archive's modern apparatus is not relicensed. Plain Shakespeare source redistribution retains Gutenberg terms; code/paper/derived numeric-data licensing does not override third-party terms. No promise of private data access on request is made.
